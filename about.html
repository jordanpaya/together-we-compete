About

Started by students. Run by students.

Why we do this

Together We Compete is a student-run initiative in Los Angeles. We started it because we kept seeing the same thing: kids who wanted to play and couldn't, because a pair of cleats or a glove was out of reach.

Access to a team, a coach, and decent gear shouldn't depend on which neighborhood you grow up in. That's the whole idea. Everything we do follows from it.

We collect equipment, sort it ourselves, and get it to schools and community organizations that already know which kids need it. We also run free sports days so there's a place to use it.
