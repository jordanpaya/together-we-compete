Donate gear

If it's still playable, we want it.

Used is fine. It needs to be clean, intact, and something a kid could use this week.

What we accept

  • Cleats and athletic shoes
  • Balls, any sport
  • Pads and protective gear
  • Helmets
  • Gloves and mitts
  • Jerseys and uniforms
  • Racquets and bats

What we can't take

  • Cracked, dented, or expired helmets
  • Broken straps, buckles, or padding
  • Mold, heavy stains, or strong odors
  • Single shoes or incomplete sets

If we'd have to throw it away, please recycle it instead.
